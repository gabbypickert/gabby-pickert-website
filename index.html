About Me

Hi, I'm
Gabby!

A singer, actress, and director based in New York City! I was raised in a little ski town in Montana where I fell in love with the outdoors and musical theatre. I have been directing and performing for as long as I can remember, and hope to continue doing so until I’m old and gray.

This May, I am graduating with my BFA in Musical Theatre from Long Island University Post. In my time at Post, I’ve trained in Suzuki, Viewpoints, Laban, and Stanislavsky acting techniques. I also learned first hand about creating new theatre while assistant directing a brand new Foreigner jukebox musical, Feels Like the First Time.

When I’m not performing, I keep up with my many hobbies including, crochet, knitting, attempting to make the world’s best chocolate chip cookie, climbing, hiking, yoga, skiing, ceramics and reading.
